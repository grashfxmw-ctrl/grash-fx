Investor services

Information and account services

Important details to review before opening or funding an account.

Deposits and withdrawals

  • Minimum deposit: $20
  • Minimum investment: $50
  • Minimum withdrawal request: $50
  • No platform deposit or withdrawal fees are currently stated.

Payment methods, processing times, verification requirements and any third-party charges must be checked before a transaction.

Referral programme

Eligible referrals can earn a $5 referral reward, subject to programme conditions.

Referral gains are held separately from the main account. Once the available referral balance reaches $60, the client can request a transfer to the main account through the client dashboard.

Account monitoring

Clients can sign in to review available account information, investment activity, referral gains and submitted financial requests.

Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved or completed. Status updates are shown through the account process.

Investor support

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about using the platform.

What is the minimum amount to deposit?

The stated minimum deposit is $20. The minimum amount to participate in an investment plan is $50. Individual plan minimums may be higher.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The stated minimum withdrawal request is $50. Requests remain subject to account verification, available balance, review and the applicable payment conditions.

Are there deposit or withdrawal fees?

GRASH FX currently states no platform deposit or withdrawal fees. Your bank, wallet provider or payment intermediary may apply separate charges.

How can I monitor my investment?

Sign in to your client dashboard to review the account information and investment activity made available to you.

How does the referral reward work?

Eligible referrals may earn a $5 reward under the referral programme rules. Referral gains can be transferred to the main account once the available referral balance reaches $60.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment returns are not guaranteed. You may lose some or all of the money you invest. Read all plan terms and risk disclosures before participating.
